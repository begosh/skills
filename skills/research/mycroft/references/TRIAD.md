# Triad — Mycroft's planning lenses

Mycroft owns the question's decomposition. Apply these lenses in his planning when the first plausible answer could anchor a complex enquiry. They propose evidence needs; they establish no research findings.

## Scrutinise the plan

| Lens | Bounded question | Planning result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanist | Which mechanisms, data flows, state changes and factual prerequisites determine the answer? | Atomic evidence needs, constraints and dependencies. |
| Adversary | Which explanations, contradictions or failure scenarios could overturn it? | Falsifiable assumptions, gaps and acceptance checks. |
| Pragmatist | Which findings change the user's decision or operational burden? | Relevant alternatives, trade-offs and optional work. |

Use the lenses within Mycroft's framing by default. When a consequential unresolved assumption warrants independent planning criticism, commission a bounded Plan Critic against the same brief and evidence horizon. Mycroft reconciles the criticism and accepts the named evidence branches, dependencies and acceptance checks.

A planning critic's job ends at the accepted plan. It does not become the Flour Spy, Oven Scout or other researcher. Start fresh evidence-runner contexts with concrete investigative commissions; independence of planning or evidence review must be reported as actually performed. Use [HOST.md](HOST.md) for access and scheduling, and [LONDON.md](LONDON.md) for the research cast.

## Reconcile

Combine mechanisms, falsification checks and decision priorities into Mycroft's brief and evidence plan. Protect the user's decisive deliverable and required missing inputs before optional work. Preserve disagreement and unknowns. Use [JEV.md](JEV.md) only if optional branches need pruning.

**Triad complete:** The plan has checkable evidence needs and acceptance criteria, and material competing explanations are retained for investigation. Subsequent [review](CRITIQUE.md) checks the returned evidence; planning criticism is a separate stage.
