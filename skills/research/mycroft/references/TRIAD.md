# Triad decomposition

Use competing viewpoints when the first plausible decomposition could anchor a complex question. The Triad proposes a plan; evidence review establishes its conclusions.

## Commission the viewpoints

| Role | Bounded question | Return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanist | Which mechanisms, data flows, state changes and factual prerequisites determine the answer? | Atomic evidence needs, constraints and dependencies. |
| Adversary | Which explanations, contradictions or failure scenarios could overturn it? | Falsifiable assumptions, gaps and acceptance checks. |
| Pragmatist | Which findings change the user's decision or operational burden? | Relevant alternatives, trade-offs and optional work. |

Give each role the same brief and evidence horizon with its own objective. Share versioned inputs, retaining independent initial analysis before sharing other roles' conclusions. Separate source-linked observations from proposed questions and hypotheses.

Use [HOST.md](HOST.md) for actual agent access, allocations and scheduling. Sequential viewpoint passes remain coordinator analysis rather than independent agents.

## Reconcile

Combine mechanisms/dependencies, falsification checks and decision priorities into the brief and evidence plan. Preserve disagreement and unknowns. Use [JEV.md](JEV.md) only if optional branches need pruning.

**Triad complete:** The plan has checkable evidence needs and acceptance criteria, and material competing explanations are retained for investigation. Subsequent [review](CRITIQUE.md) checks the returned evidence; planning criticism is a separate stage.
