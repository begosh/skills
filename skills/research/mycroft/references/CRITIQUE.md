# The Diogenes Review (Adversarial Critique & Quarantine)

The Critic is the firewall between specialist investigations and final publication. His duty is adversarial cross-examination.

---

## 1. The Four Non-Negotiable Audit Checks

1. **Entailment & Unauthorized Inference Check**:
   - Compares the specialist's claim word-for-word against the primary source line.
   - Rejects unauthorized adjectives (e.g. "lukewarm water" when source says "water").
   - Rejects uncalculated additions (e.g. baker's percentages when prompt said no calculations).

2. **The Non-Corroboration Law**:
   - *"Never treat two agents' agreement as proof."*
   - Shared model hallucination is common. Truth is measured exclusively against primary source locators and exact excerpts, never agent consensus.

3. **Chronological & Dependency Integrity Check**:
   - Verifies that procedural actions match physical reality and source sequence.
   - Ensures preparation stages (bulk ferment, balling, proofing, stretching) maintain causality.

4. **Missing Information Protection**:
   - Audits that all unstated conditions in the source remain documented in `gaps`.
   - Forbids specialists or synthesizers from quietly filling gaps from training memory.

---

## 2. Bounded Execution Repair Loop

When the Critic returns `FAIL`:
1. **Max Retries**: Exactly **2 repair cycles** (3 attempts max).
2. **Rephrase Objective**: Mycroft feeds the Critic's specific defect note into the specialist's prompt:
   - *"Your previous return was rejected: you inferred 'lukewarm' when source states temperature is unstated. Rerun extraction strictly without inferring temperature."*
3. **Immutable Contracts**: Acceptance checks cannot be loosened to force a pass.

---

## 3. Quarantine Protocol

If a specialist cannot satisfy the Critic after 2 repair attempts, or if an external finding is materially disputed by a conflicting primary source:
* The claim is **QUARANTINED**.
* It is stripped from the main executive summary.
* It is placed in the **Quarantined Findings Appendix** with the explicit reason and failing attempt trace.
* The run proceeds to synthesis without crashing.
